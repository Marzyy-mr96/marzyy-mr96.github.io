Civil engineering researcher · University of Kansas

Data engineering, analytics, and science for disaster resilience.

I’m a Ph.D. researcher in Civil and Environmental Engineering.

I build reliable data pipelines, analyze long-term recovery patterns, and apply statistical modeling and machine learning to understand how communities recover after disasters. My background in structural reliability keeps uncertainty and careful validation at the center of that work.

Research interests

  • AI for disaster resilience

    Machine learning that helps explain long-term recovery and is trustworthy enough to inform real decisions.

  • Long-term housing recovery

    How households and housing recover over years, especially when disasters strike the same community more than once.

  • Data engineering

    Building reliable, reproducible pipelines that collect, clean, and integrate survey, hazard, and census data for analysis.

  • Data science for disaster data

    Turning incomplete, multi-year, multi-source field data into reliable, reproducible evidence.

  • Structural engineering

    Understanding how structures respond to loads through nonlinear analysis, structural reliability, and uncertainty quantification.

  • Equity, risk, and uncertainty

    Who falls behind after disasters, and how uncertainty shapes what models can support, from structures to communities.

Selected highlights

  • Doctoral research on AI and long-term recovery after repeated flooding, using the NIST-funded Lumberton longitudinal field study
  • Built reproducible Python workflows linking multi-wave household surveys, field inspections, and public hazard and census data
  • Implemented time-series clustering (Dynamic Time Warping) and latent class growth models in Python to identify recovery pathways
  • Designed leakage-safe validation for machine-learning models and caught a data-leakage problem that had inflated early results
  • First-author journal manuscript under review at PLOS ONE
  • Recipient of a Society for Risk Analysis travel award
