Structural Engineering

Structural Analysis and Design Projects

Finite-element modeling in Python and dynamic and nonlinear static analyses of buildings, alongside steel and reinforced concrete design.

Overview

Alongside my M.Sc. research, I worked on structural engineering projects that combined programming with conventional analysis and design.

What I did

  • Modeled structures with finite-element methods written in Python.
  • Performed response-spectrum, time-history, and pushover (nonlinear static) analyses.
  • Worked on steel and reinforced concrete design, including beam and connection design, and on structural programming that accounts for inelastic behavior.

Skills demonstrated

Finite-element modeling, seismic analysis, nonlinear structural behavior, and engineering software.
